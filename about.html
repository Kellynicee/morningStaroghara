Welcome to MorningStar Covenant International Church Otefe Oghara

ABOUT US

MorningStar Covenant Int'l Church is a leading Church in Warri, Delta State, Nigeria. It is a vibrant Word-based, Christ-centred, lively, Spirit-filled and Spirit-led Church where Jesus is unarguably Lord. Our passion is soul-winning and cultivation, and we do this in a spiritually conducive atmosphere of real fellowship with believers.

We believe and engage the power of discipleship to raise and train men who will be mightily used by God, just as our Lord Jesus did during His life here on earth. (Mark 3:13-15)

We have been called to host the revival for this region and beyond. The Lord said to us, “from this place will sound forth the Word..."

If you are in need of a Christian Community to truly call home, then MorningStar is the Church for you.

We are a supernatural people, we have no taste for religion without change. We live a result-oriented, purpose-driven life based on principles in God's Word.

We are a people of the Word, yielded to the Spirit and committed to God's purpose for our lives. We take our place in God's supernatural army and His agenda for the earth in our generation.

As God helps us, we will never give up, we will never give in, we will not quit, we will not die, until our job is done and victory is enforced.
